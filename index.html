about me

Not a rockstar. Just someone who ships.

I'm Kaif — somewhere after 18, AI engineer from Muzaffarnagar, UP. Currently doing BCA at Shree Ram Group of College. Before that, an ordinary kid from an ordinary school who found Python in 2022 and never shut up about it since.

I build voice assistants, RAG pipelines, and agentic systems. Most of my learning came from breaking things in my own projects — not from courses or certificates. J.A.R.V.I.S and kairos.ai exist because I wanted them to exist.

I write code mostly at night. I read papers I barely understand. I ship things nobody asked for. And once in a while, something actually works.

Building AI that doesn't just respond — but reasons, remembers, and acts. Still figuring out 90% of it.
